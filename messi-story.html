Lionel Messi: big moments

From Barcelona to the World Cup.

Three moments worth remembering

1. Eight Ballon d'Or awards

Messi won a record eight Ballon d'Or awards. What makes him special is not just scoring: he can decide a match with a pass or a run through midfield.

2. The World Cup in Qatar

In December 2022, Messi captained Argentina to the World Cup after a dramatic final against France. He scored twice in the final and also converted in the shootout.

3. The Barcelona years

Messi came through Barcelona's youth system and became the centre of its attack. His combination play, dribbling and finishing helped define an era at the club.
