Cristiano Ronaldo: big moments

Big goals and even bigger nights.

Three moments worth remembering

1. The five Ballon d'Ors

Ronaldo claimed five Ballon d'Or awards between 2008 and 2017. His best years combined pace, long-range shooting and relentless goalscoring.

2. Those Champions League nights

He won the Champions League with Manchester United in 2008 and lifted it four more times at Real Madrid. His movement in the box and finishing were huge weapons in knockout ties.

3. Portugal finally did it

At Euro 2016, Portugal beat France in the final. Ronaldo went off injured during the match, but the trophy was a defining moment in his international career.
